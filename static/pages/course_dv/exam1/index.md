# Exam 1 Review

Exam 1 will be 80% conceptual, and 20% practical. Review the core concepts from our class, and work on how to apply these concepts to practical situations. The applied portion will be done with Tableau. See quiz 1 for an example.

Structure:

- Definitions, see flashcards 
- Multiple-choice, see pre-built questions for each module
- Application, I will provide examples of visualizations and ask you for relevant terms and concepts. I will also ask you to critique them based off of the class principles.
- Tableau file, I will provide you with a dataset and ask you to create a visualization based on the data. You will be graded on your ability to apply the concepts we have learned in class.
