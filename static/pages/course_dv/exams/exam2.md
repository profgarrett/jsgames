# Exam 2 Review

Exam 2 is focused on Tableau. You should apply the lessons-learned from module 1 of the class, but there will be no traditional multiple-choice or short-answer questions.

Structure:

- Tableau file, I will provide you with a dataset and ask you to create a visualization based on the data. You will be graded on your ability to apply the concepts we have learned in class.
