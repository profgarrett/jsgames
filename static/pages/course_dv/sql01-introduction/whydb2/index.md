# Problem: Why use a database instead of Excel?

## Outcome

- Identify reasons to use a relational database instead of Excel

## Problem

ABC Woodworking has asked you to analyze sales records. They want invest $20,000 into a new Google Adwords campaign, which their CMO thinks will boost sales by 20%. However, they want to maximize their return on investment.

What one product should they promote to maximize their investment?

## Process
- Download the [problem file](problem.csv) and load it into Excel
- Open the file in Excel
- Examine the data for potential data problems. Try to look at reasons for a DB, and see if you can find any problems in the Excel file.
